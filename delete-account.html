Delete Your Account

Permanently remove your Solidten account and all associated data.

Delete directly in the app

The fastest way to delete your account is directly inside the Solidten app:

  • 1Open the Solidten app
  • 2Go to your Profile tab (bottom right)
  • 3Tap Settings
  • 4Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  • 5Confirm in the dialog — deletion is immediate
⚠️

Deletion is permanent and immediate. Your profile, photos, all ratings given, and all ratings received are removed. This cannot be undone.
