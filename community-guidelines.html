Community Guidelines

Last updated: May 31, 2026

🤝

Solidten is built on honesty, respect, and mutual consent. These guidelines exist to make sure every person on this platform feels safe, treated fairly, and free to be themselves. Please take a few minutes to read them — they apply to everyone, without exception.

🔞

Solidten is strictly for users aged 18 and over. Creating an account on behalf of a minor, or misrepresenting your age, is a serious violation that results in immediate and permanent account termination.

Our Community Values

Solidten is a place where adults can participate in honest, appearance-based ratings in a consensual and controlled environment. Everyone who joins does so knowingly and voluntarily. That shared understanding is what makes this community work — and it's also what makes respecting each other non-negotiable.

We expect every member to treat others the way they'd want to be treated: honestly, but with dignity. A low rating delivered with respect is acceptable. Cruelty is not.

1. Respectful Communication

All interactions on Solidten — including ratings, messages, and any other form of communication — must be conducted with basic human respect.

  • Treat every person on this platform as you would a stranger in public — with common courtesy, even if you disagree with them or find them unattractive.
  • Constructive, honest feedback is welcome. Deliberate cruelty, humiliation, or targeted mockery is not.
  • Disagreements should stay proportionate and civil. Escalating minor conflicts into personal attacks is a violation of these guidelines.
  • No communication should be designed to make another user feel worthless, degraded, or unsafe.

2. Authentic and Accurate Profiles

The integrity of Solidten depends on every profile being genuine. Fake or misleading profiles undermine trust for everyone.

  • Be yourself. Use your real photos, real gender identity, and accurate location. Profiles that misrepresent who you are will be removed.
  • One account per person. Creating multiple accounts to game ratings, evade a ban, or manipulate others is a violation of these guidelines. If identified, duplicate or evasion accounts will be terminated.
  • Your photos must be of you. Do not use photos of other people, celebrities, AI-generated images of someone else, or stock photos as your profile photos.
  • No impersonation. Do not create a profile that pretends to be, or could be confused with, another real person — whether a public figure or a private individual.
  • Stay current. Photos should represent how you look today. Heavily outdated or significantly altered photos are misleading to other users.

3. Age Requirements

Solidten is an adults-only platform. This is not a guideline preference — it is a hard rule enforced at every level of the service.

  • You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, use the app, or appear on the platform in any capacity.
  • Do not upload photos that include minors — this includes photos where a child appears in the background.
  • If you encounter a profile you suspect belongs to a minor, report it immediately using the in-app reporting tool. We treat these reports as the highest-priority moderation cases.
  • Accounts found to belong to users under 18 are terminated immediately and permanently, and the incident may be reported to the relevant authorities.

4. Harassment, Bullying, and Hate Speech

We have zero tolerance for harassment, coordinated attacks, or speech that targets people based on who they are.

Harassment and bullying

  • Do not send repeated unwanted messages to someone who has not responded or has indicated they are not interested.
  • Do not use the platform to intimidate, threaten, humiliate, or demean another person.
  • Do not organise or participate in coordinated efforts to mass-report, abuse, or drive a specific user off the platform.
  • Receiving a low rating does not justify retaliatory behaviour toward the person who gave it. Ratings are anonymous for a reason.

Hate speech

  • Content that degrades, dehumanises, or threatens people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics is not permitted anywhere on this platform.
  • Slurs, derogatory stereotypes, and statements that incite hatred or discrimination are strictly prohibited.
  • This applies to profile content, messages, and any other form of expression within the app.

5. Inappropriate and Explicit Content

Solidten is an appearance-based platform, but that does not mean anything goes with photos or content.

  • No explicit nudity or sexual content. Profile photos must not include nudity, sexually explicit imagery, or graphic sexual acts. Tasteful, non-explicit photos are acceptable.
  • No graphic violence. Do not upload photos depicting real or simulated violence, gore, or self-harm.
  • No illegal content. Uploading, sharing, or distributing content that is illegal in your jurisdiction — or in the jurisdiction where our servers operate — is strictly prohibited and may be reported to law enforcement.
  • No content involving minors in a sexual context, under any circumstances. This is an absolute and non-negotiable rule. Violations will be reported to the relevant authorities immediately.

Our automated moderation system uses Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch to scan every uploaded photo. Content flagged as adult, violent, or explicitly sexual is automatically removed. Manual review by our moderation team follows for accounts that trigger repeated flags.

6. Privacy and Personal Information

Everyone has a right to control what personal information they share. Respect that right — both for yourself and for others.

Your own information

  • Be thoughtful about what you share publicly. Profile information, photos, and location details are visible to other registered users.
  • You can adjust your privacy settings at any time in the app, including whether your rating score is publicly visible.

Other people's information

  • Do not share another user's personal information — including their name, photos, location, contact details, or anything else they have shared on this platform — outside of the app without their explicit consent. This practice, commonly known as "doxxing," is a serious violation.
  • Screenshots of profiles or conversations shared publicly or maliciously are a violation of user trust and these guidelines.
  • Do not attempt to identify anonymous raters, or use any information available in the app to track, locate, or make contact with another user outside of the app.
  • Do not use information obtained through Solidten for commercial purposes, data collection, or any form of stalking or surveillance.

7. Ratings and Feedback

Rating is the core mechanic of Solidten, and it comes with responsibilities.

  • Ratings are meant to reflect your honest, personal assessment of physical attractiveness — nothing more. They are not a tool for punishment, revenge, or manipulation.
  • Do not coordinate with others to mass-rate a specific profile in a particular direction to distort their score unfairly.
  • Ratings are anonymous. Do not attempt to guess or expose who gave a particular rating.
  • Receiving a rating you disagree with is a normal part of using this platform. Reacting to it with hostility toward other users is not acceptable.

8. Reporting and Moderation

Our moderation team works to keep Solidten safe, but we rely heavily on the community to flag violations we might not otherwise see.

  • Use the report button. Every profile has an in-app report option. If you see something that violates these guidelines, please use it. Reports are anonymous and reviewed by a human moderator.
  • Report in good faith. The reporting system exists to protect the community — not to weaponise against users you simply dislike or have received a low rating from. Repeated bad-faith reports are themselves a violation.
  • What happens after a report:
    • A single report flags the profile for review and triggers an email notification to our moderation team.
    • Reports involving content that is clearly underage, sexually explicit, or constitutes extortion or blackmail trigger immediate profile hiding, regardless of how many reports have been filed.
    • After 3 distinct user reports, a profile is automatically hidden from the rating queue pending review.
    • After 5 distinct user reports, the account is suspended and the user is unable to log in until a moderation review is completed.
  • Moderation decisions are final unless a clear error has been made. If you believe your account was actioned in error, contact us at solid10.support@gmail.com.

9. Account Suspension and Bans

We take enforcement seriously. Violations of these guidelines result in consequences proportionate to the severity of the breach.

Temporary suspension

Accounts that accumulate 5 or more reports from distinct users are automatically suspended pending a moderation review. During this time, the user cannot log in. If the review determines the reports were unfounded, the account is reinstated with no penalty.

Permanent ban

The following violations result in an immediate and permanent ban, with no possibility of appeal:

  • Any content involving minors in a sexual context
  • Threats of physical violence toward another user
  • Blackmail or extortion using content shared on the platform
  • Creating accounts to evade a previous ban
  • Coordinated abuse campaigns against specific users
  • Impersonating a real person or operating a fake identity for deceptive purposes

Permanently banned accounts have their Firebase Authentication login disabled. In serious cases, particularly those involving illegal content or threats of violence, we may share relevant information with law enforcement.

No ban evasion

Attempting to circumvent a suspension or ban by creating a new account is a violation in its own right. If identified, the new account will be terminated and the original enforcement action upheld.

10. Community Expectations

Beyond the specific rules above, we ask every member of Solidten to hold themselves to a general standard of good-faith participation.

  • Participate for the right reasons. Solidten is designed for genuine, personal engagement — not for marketing, data harvesting, research, or commercial activity of any kind.
  • Don't exploit the platform. Any attempt to manipulate ratings, automate interactions, reverse-engineer the app, or exploit technical vulnerabilities is prohibited and may result in legal action.
  • Accept that not everyone will like you. A platform built around honest ratings means some ratings will sting. That's inherent to the concept. The community works best when members can handle honest feedback with grace.
  • Be the kind of user you'd want to interact with. The atmosphere of this community is shaped by every individual in it. Your choices matter.

11. Updates to These Guidelines

We may update these Community Guidelines from time to time as the platform evolves. Significant changes will be communicated through the app. Continued use of Solidten after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised guidelines.

If you have questions about these guidelines, or want to report a concern that doesn't fit the in-app reporting flow, contact us at solid10.support@gmail.com.
