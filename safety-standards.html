Child Safety Standards

Last updated: June 3, 2026

🛡️

Solidten is committed to maintaining a safe platform and has a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Age Requirement

Solidten is strictly for users aged 18 and older. Users are required to confirm they are at least 18 years old during registration. Accounts found to belong to minors are immediately terminated.

Zero Tolerance for CSAE

Solidten has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any content that sexually exploits minors. Any such content will be removed immediately and reported to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable.

Reporting Mechanisms

All users can report profiles or content that they believe violates our child safety standards directly within the app. Every profile on the platform includes a report button. Reports are reviewed promptly and appropriate action is taken.

Content Moderation

We actively monitor our platform for content that violates our policies. Accounts in violation of our child safety standards are permanently banned.
